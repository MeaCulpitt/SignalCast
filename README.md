# SignalCast: The Targeted Amplification Subnet

SignalCast is a decentralized **Market Intelligence & Targeted Amplification Layer** built on Bittensor. It is designed to leverage the outputs of **Subnet 93 (Bitcast)** by routing AI-generated advertising content to specific customer groups including high-value industrial targets. 

Unlike traditional advertising networks that optimize for simple impressions, SignalCast optimizes for **Precision Engagement and Conversion**.

---

## 🚀 Overview & Mission

By decentralizing the logic of precision targeting, SignalCast amplifies high-quality creative assets by routing them to verified corporate audiences. The subnet transforms traditional digital advertising into an **Intelligent Distribution Engine**, incentivizing a global network of miners to identify and reach decision-makers within specific industrial sectors using real-time technographic signals and interest triggers.

---

## 💡 Key Technical Pillars

* **Precision Targeting:** Shifting focus from broad reach to high-resolution targeting of specific corporate accounts based on technographic profiles and real-time interest signals (such as software migrations or performance bottlenecks).
* **The Interest Pipeline:** A tiered reward system that tracks the user journey from initial social awareness (Likes/Follows) to high-intent industrial actions (Whitepaper downloads, Demo signups, and Contact Form submissions).
* **Firmographic Auditing:** Validators perform Reverse IP and ASN analysis to ensure every TAO of emission is backed by a verified corporate lead rather than residential or bot traffic.

## 🛠 High-Level Architecture: The Interest Pipeline

SignalCast miners bridge the gap between social awareness and industrial intent through a four-stage loop:

1. **Strategic Discovery:** Miners identify "Pulse Zones" where relevant corporate decision-makers are active.
2. **Engagement (Social Interest):** High-authority targets interact with the amplified asset (Likes, Follows, Reposts).
3. **Conversion (Industrial Intent):** Targets follow SignalCast-tracked links to perform high-value actions:
   * **Resource Ingest:** Downloading technical Whitepapers or Case Studies.
   * **Product Evaluation:** Signing up for live Demos.
   * **Direct Inquiry:** Submitting Contact Forms for procurement.
4. **Validation:** Validators cross-reference these events with Reverse IP telemetry and the SN93 data loop to issue rewards based on targeting accuracy.

## ⚖️ Governance & Slashing

SignalCast maintains a "Zero-Trust" environment. Miners who attempt to forge attribution data or push low-quality bot traffic are subject to immediate stake burning via the **Honey-Link Telemetry** audit, which detects non-human interaction patterns and ensures the integrity of the amplification layer.
