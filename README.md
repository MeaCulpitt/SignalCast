# SignalCast: The Adversarial Distribution Subnet

SignalCast is a decentralized **Market Intelligence & Distribution Layer** built on Bittensor. It is designed to scale the impact of **Subnet 93 (Bitcast)** by routing AI-generated content to high-value industrial targets. 

Unlike traditional advertising networks that optimize for impressions, SignalCast optimizes for **Competitive Displacement**.

---

## 🚀 Overview & Mission

SignalCast is the **Adversarial Distribution Layer** for the Bittensor network. It bridges the gap between AI content generation (**Subnet 93 / Bitcast**) and industrial market impact. By decentralizing the logic of competitive conquesting, SignalCast turns high-quality creative assets into verified corporate interest, specifically targeting the customer bases of industry incumbents. 

It transforms the "spray and pray" model of B2B marketing into a high-precision **Conquest Engine**, incentivizing a global network of miners to identify, track, and "snipe" the customer bases of industry incumbents using real-time technographic signals and dissatisfaction triggers.

---

## 💡 Key Technical Pillars

* **Strategic Conquesting:** Shifting focus from broad reach to "sniping" competitor customers based on real-time dissatisfaction triggers—such as service outages, price hikes, or technical debt—and technographic ruptures.
* **The Interest Pipeline:** A tiered reward system that tracks the user journey from social engagement (Likes/Follows) to high-intent industrial actions (Whitepaper downloads, Demo signups, and Contact Form submissions).
* **Firmographic Auditing:** Validators perform Reverse IP and ASN analysis to ensure every TAO of emission is backed by a verified corporate lead rather than residential or bot traffic.

## 🛠 High-Level Architecture: The Interest Pipeline

SignalCast miners bridge the gap between social awareness and industrial intent through a four-stage loop:

1. **Strategic Discovery:** Miners identify "Pulse Zones" where competitor customers are active.
2. **Engagement (Social Interest):** High-authority targets interact with the asset (Likes, Follows, Reposts).
3. **Conversion (Industrial Intent):** Customers follow SignalCast-tracked links to perform high-value actions:
   * **Resource Ingest:** Downloading technical Whitepapers or Case Studies.
   * **Product Evaluation:** Signing up for live Demos.
   * **Direct Inquiry:** Submitting Contact Forms for procurement.
4. **Validation:** Validators cross-reference these events with Reverse IP telemetry and the SN93 data loop to issue "Conquest" rewards.

## ⚖️ Governance & Slashing

SignalCast maintains a "Zero-Trust" environment. Miners who attempt to forge attribution data or push low-quality bot traffic are subject to immediate stake burning via the **Honey-Link Telemetry** audit, which detects non-human interaction patterns.
