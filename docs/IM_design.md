# SignalCast: Incentive & Mechanism Design

SignalCast is an intelligent distribution layer that bridges the gap between AI content creation (**Subnet 93 / Bitcast**) and real-world industrial impact. The mechanism is designed to transform anonymous social media reach into verified corporate interest and competitive market share.

---

### Emission and Reward Logic

The SignalCast emission schedule follows a **Strategic Tiering** model that prioritizes "High-Intent Signal" over "Raw Reach." Emissions are weighted based on the **Authority** of the audience and the **Depth** of their engagement.

* **Tier 1: Standard Performance (40%):** Distributed based on organic conversion and engagement (clicks, watch-time) across general niche audiences.
* **Tier 2: Competitive Conquesting (30%):** A **2x to 5x multiplier** is applied to engagement originating from verified corporate domains of **Target Competitors** or their **Major Customers**. 
* **Tier 3: Lead Conversion (30%):** Rewards are issued for verified "Interest Signals" tracked via the SN93 data loop:
    * **Social Interest:** X.com Likes, Reposts, and new "Follows" to the advertiser’s profile.
    * **Educational Intent:** Downloads of technical Whitepapers or Case Studies.
    * **Direct Action:** Demo Signups and Contact Form submissions.
* **Technographic Bounties:** A dynamic pool of emissions is reserved for miners who are the first to detect and act upon "Market Ruptures," such as a competitor's software churn or an End-of-Life (EOL) technology window.

---

### Incentive Alignment for Miners and Validators

The mechanism ensures that all participants profit only when the advertiser—and by extension, the Bitcast ecosystem—sees a measurable ROI.

* **Miners (Strategic Snipers):** Miners are incentivized to perform **Digital Reconnaissance**. Because rewards are weighted toward high-value corporate domains and "Lead Magnets" (Whitepapers/Demos), they must identify "Pulse Zones" (gated forums, industrial Slack groups, X.com threads) where decision-makers gather.
* **Validators (Verification Oracles):** Validators act as **Firmographic Oracles**. Their own dividends depend on their ability to accurately distinguish between a high-value corporate lead and residential bot noise. By cross-referencing visitor telemetry with the SN93 conversion loop, they ensure emissions are backed by genuine intent.
* **The Cross-Subnet Synergy:** By utilizing the SN93 (Bitcast) output, SignalCast increases the demand for high-quality creative assets, raising the value of both subnets' Alpha tokens through a feedback loop of creation and high-precision distribution.

---

### Mechanisms to Discourage Low-Quality or Adversarial Behavior

SignalCast maintains a "Zero-Trust" environment through **Economic Deterrence** and **Behavioral Analysis**.

* **Stake-at-Risk (Slashing):** Miners must stake Alpha/TAO to participate in premium briefs. If a miner's traffic is flagged as fraudulent or bot-driven (verified by lack of "Human Signatures"), their stake is burned.
* **Honey-Link Telemetry:** Validators "wrap" distributed links in invisible telemetry layers. If a click occurs without human-like behavioral variance (e.g., linear scroll velocity or lack of cursor jitter), it is disqualified.
* **Saturation Throttling:** To protect an advertiser's brand reputation, validators apply frequency caps. If a miner "spams" a single target company or domain, their rewards for that account are zeroed out.

---

### How this design qualifies as "Proof of Intelligence"

SignalCast qualifies as a **Proof of Intelligence** subnet by requiring complex, non-algorithmic decision-making that cannot be solved by brute-force hardware.

* **Proof of Intelligence (Market Mapping):** Miners must use LLMs and predictive modeling to "map" a competitor’s customer base and identify technographic vulnerabilities. Matching a specific SN93 creative asset to a "Decision Window" (like an active RFP or tech-stack migration) requires sophisticated semantic reasoning.
* **Proof of Effort (Operational Grit):** The continuous scouting of fragmented "Dark Social" networks, maintaining niche distribution channels, and performing real-time technographic detection represents a verifiable expenditure of time and strategic resource.

---

### High-Level Algorithm: The Interest Pipeline

SignalCast miners bridge the gap between social awareness and industrial intent. Potential customers discover "Lead Magnets" (Whitepapers, Demos, Contact Forms) through the following loop:

1. **Task Ingest & Discovery:** Validators pull active SN93 assets, TALs, and Lead Magnets. Miners place these assets in front of "problem-aware" audiences on X.com, industry forums, and gated communities.
2. **Engagement (Social Interest):** High-authority targets interact with the asset (Likes, Follows, Reposts). Validators verify these via social APIs, triggering a Tier 2 reward.
3. **Conversion (Industrial Intent):** Users follow the SignalCast-tracked link to the advertiser's landing page and perform high-value actions:
   * **Resource Ingest:** Downloading technical Whitepapers or Case Studies.
   * **Product Evaluation:** Signing up for live Demos.
   * **Direct Inquiry:** Submitting Contact Forms for procurement.
4. **Validation (The Audit):** Validators cross-reference these off-chain events with the SN93 data loop and Reverse IP lookups to verify the lead's firmographic authority and issue Tier 3 "Conquest" rewards.
5. **Scoring & Allocation:** Validators calculate the final score based on the **Conquest Multiplier** and **Conversion Depth**. Yuma Consensus processes these weights to allocate block rewards.
